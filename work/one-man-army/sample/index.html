How a signal earns its stage

rules, never the model
  • CandidateOne learning at E2 or above.
  • EmergingThree learnings at E2+, from two calls, within 90 days.
  • ValidatedTwo learnings at E4+, from two calls, within 180 days.
  • FadingIts newest support has aged. Nothing is deleted.

Grades say how solid a statement is — E2 names a number, a date or a name; E3 states an effect; E5 is a signed commitment. Outcomes the hub exports back never count towards maturity: calibration is not proof.

One thing never travels this path: a partner mentions a quality complaint on a call — that goes into the hub's own lot and safety flows, raised by the founder. Evidence is never a safety source.

Next: the gate it must pass
Step 5 of 6 · Monday, 09:24

Advice is not an effect. The gate decides that.

The founder approved the board's plan — signed with the hardware key, bound to exactly what was on screen. What follows is the same six steps every action takes, whether it is a £12 refund or a £4,000 reorder. Money actions echo to the founder and hold for ten minutes, so they can be stopped.

Next: the week closes
Step 6 of 6 · Friday, 17:00

The loop closes. Nothing validates itself.

On Friday the verdict goes back to the evidence plane — an export the founder approved, verdicts as ids and amounts, no names, no message text. It is used to calibrate predictions only: an outcome never counts towards a signal's maturity, so a board can never mark its own advice as proven.

The Link B export

approved · downloads once · no push

A push would be a new way out of the hub — Architecture B's relay, by trigger only. So the founder downloads it and uploads it by hand. It is stored, visible and citable on the other side, and excluded from every metric.

    The design · v1 and v2

    Thirteen steps. One way out.

    Architecture A is the minimum complete company operating system: forty-six components, drawn as thirteen numbered steps. Architecture B is the same company with every at-scale part switched on — each waits for its own measured trigger behind an interface A already has, so nothing is thrown away.

    Architecture A, at a glance

    46 components · A01–A46

    Work comes in through one front door (02–05), is planned and done in the middle (06–09), and leaves through one gate (10); every result comes back as a record (12). Learning (13) is how anything changes. The full pages, master and build, open live in a meeting — these are the same thirteen steps, redrawn small.
