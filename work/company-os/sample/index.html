The lane that never stops

protective · sent

A reaction arrived while the switch was down. The customer was acknowledged and the pharmacist told anyway — protective steps are the one thing the kill switch never pauses.

Why the exception exists

Safety work never waits for a model — and it never waits for the switch either. Everything else stops: replies, order updates, warehouse writes. The lane that protects a customer keeps going.

That is the contrast the project page states as a benchmark: seen within 10 s; nothing sent after — 287 ms; 0 sent.
