About the eight names

Simulations, not the people.

Eight angles on the same decision. Each advisor brings one question to every meeting.

The advisors are simulations. Each one is built only from a well-known person's public work, such as books, shareholder letters, essays, talks and interviews, to show how that kind of thinker weighs a decision. They are not those people, and they do not speak for them.

None of the eight is connected with Indica Technology or with the Board Room, and none of them has reviewed, approved or endorsed it. Their names are used only to say whose public work a seat is built from. Anything a seat says in this sample is the simulation's words, never the real person's.

The bank pilot and the GPU decision are samples written to test the board. No real company is behind them, and nothing you click here leaves your browser.
