      Threads

      One thread per company. Dormant is computed: an open thread with no call for 90 days. Won and lost are yours to mark; nothing moves a thread for you.

      Signals

      A signal is one thing several calls say. Its stage comes from rules over grades, calls and dates, never from the model: 3 learnings at E2 or above from 2 calls in 90 days make it Emerging; 2 at E4 or above from 2 calls in 180 days make it Validated.

      Positions

      Open decisions drafted from your calls. You give each option a probability and argue against yourself; the label says whether the evidence backs it.

      People & companies

      Names said in calls. Two names become one only when you merge them, and a merge can be undone.
