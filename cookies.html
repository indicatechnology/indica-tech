Cookie Policy

The short version: indica-tech.com sets no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. It stores one thing in your browser — the currency you chose to see prices in — and nothing else. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. The long version, for completeness, is below.

Version 1.1 Effective 29 September 2026

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website can ask your browser to store. Cookies can hold session information ("you are logged in"), preferences ("you chose dark mode"), or tracking identifiers ("you are the same visitor who came back"). Under UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, PECR), websites must get consent before storing anything on your device that is not strictly necessary for the service you requested. PECR covers more than cookies — other browser storage counts too, which is why the next section names the one thing we store.

Cookies this site sets

None. indica-tech.com is a static marketing site. It does not set first-party cookies for analytics, advertising, or behavioural tracking. We chose this deliberately — if we are going to ask you for a five-figure engagement we should not be the site that drops 20 tracking pixels on you.

What this site does store in your browser

One item, and only if you use the currency switch in the navigation bar:

Name Type What it is for How long it lasts
indica_region Local storage — not a cookie Remembers whether you asked to see prices in GBP, USD or EUR, so your choice survives when you move to another page. Until you clear your browser storage.

It holds one of three values — “uk”, “us” or “eu”. It contains no identifier, it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it never leaves your browser. It is a preference you set deliberately, in the same category as choosing dark mode, so it is exempt from the PECR consent requirement. Clearing your site data removes it and the site works exactly as before.

If you never touch the currency switch, nothing is stored at all. The site picks a currency from your browser's own time-zone setting, which it reads on your device and does not record or transmit.

What about the third-party services linked from this site?

This site embeds a small number of third-party resources. Some may set their own cookies when you interact with them. We disclose them here so you can make an informed choice:

Third party When it applies What they do
Google Fonts On every page — loads IBM Plex typefaces. Serves font files. Google may log the HTTP request (IP address, user agent). See Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
FormSubmit Only when you submit the contact form. Delivers the form submission to our inbox, and shows an anti-spam captcha step on its own domain before accepting it. Any cookies involved are set there, not here. See FormSubmit privacy.
Cal.com Only when you click through to book a discovery call. Scheduling. Cal.com sets its own cookies on its own domain — not ours. See Cal.com privacy.
YouTube (Google) Only when you click play on one of the video panels on our home page. Until you click, the panel is drawn by us and makes no request to Google at all — no player, no thumbnail, no beacon. Clicking loads the player from youtube-nocookie.com, Google's reduced-tracking domain, which may then set cookies and receive your IP address. See Google privacy.
LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Only if you click a social link in our footer or content section. Each social network sets its own cookies on its own domain once you arrive there. Nothing happens on our domain from the link itself.

None of these third parties set cookies via this site unless you interact with them. Google Fonts is the one exception in that it is requested on every page load, which is why it is listed first. None of them are used for cross-site tracking or advertising on our behalf.

How to control cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you block, delete, or audit cookies. We recommend it as a general good habit even though we do not set any ourselves:

  • Chrome — manage cookies
  • Firefox — clear cookies
  • Safari — manage cookies
  • Edge — delete cookies

Blocking cookies globally will have zero effect on your experience of this site, because we do not rely on any.

What if this changes?

If we ever add analytics, session recording, A/B testing, or any other cookie-setting tool in the future, we will update this page first and add a proper consent banner before collecting anything. The effective date at the top of the page tells you when this document last changed.

Spotted a cookie we missed?

We want this page to stay accurate. If you see a cookie set by this site that is not listed above, email hello@indica-tech.com and we will investigate and update within 48 hours.
