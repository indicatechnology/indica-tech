Cookie Policy

The short version: indica-tech.com does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. The long version, for completeness, is below.

Effective 22 April 2026 Version 1.0

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website can ask your browser to store. Cookies can hold session information ("you are logged in"), preferences ("you chose dark mode"), or tracking identifiers ("you are the same visitor who came back"). Under UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, PECR), websites must get consent before setting any cookie that is not strictly necessary for the service you requested.

Cookies this site sets

None. indica-tech.com is a static marketing site. It does not set first-party cookies for analytics, advertising, or behavioural tracking. We chose this deliberately — if we are going to ask you for £45,000 we should not be the site that drops 20 tracking pixels on you.

What about the third-party services linked from this site?

This site embeds a small number of third-party resources. Some may set their own cookies when you interact with them. We disclose them here so you can make an informed choice:

Third party When it applies What they do
Google Fonts On every page — loads IBM Plex typefaces. Serves font files. Google may log the HTTP request (IP address, user agent). See Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
FormSubmit Only when you submit the contact form. Delivers the form submission to our inbox. See FormSubmit privacy.
Cal.com Only when you click through to book a discovery call. Scheduling. Cal.com sets its own cookies on its own domain — not ours. See Cal.com privacy.
LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Only if you click a social link in our footer or content section. Each social network sets its own cookies on its own domain once you arrive there. Nothing happens on our domain from the link itself.

None of these third parties set cookies via this site unless you interact with them. They are not used for cross-site tracking or advertising on our behalf.

How to control cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you block, delete, or audit cookies. We recommend it as a general good habit even though we do not set any ourselves:

  • Chrome — manage cookies
  • Firefox — clear cookies
  • Safari — manage cookies
  • Edge — delete cookies

Blocking cookies globally will have zero effect on your experience of this site, because we do not rely on any.

What if this changes?

If we ever add analytics, session recording, A/B testing, or any other cookie-setting tool in the future, we will update this page first and add a proper consent banner before collecting anything. The effective date at the top of the page tells you when this document last changed.

Spotted a cookie we missed?

We want this page to stay accurate. If you see a cookie set by this site that is not listed above, email hello@indica-tech.com and we will investigate and update within 48 hours.
